# Manuscript Digitization and Restoration

## Overview

Manuscript Digitization and Restoration is a project focused on
preserving Nepali manuscripts by converting manuscript material
into digital form and improving its usability for digital
preservation and examination.

The project explores how technology can support the preservation
of historical and cultural written material.

## Problem Statement

Historical manuscripts can present challenges for digital
preservation, access, and examination. A digital workflow can
help preserve manuscript content and make it easier to inspect
and work with the material.

## Project Objectives

- Support the digitization of Nepali manuscript material.
- Explore restoration techniques appropriate to the source material.
- Improve the usability of digitized manuscripts.
- Investigate technical approaches for preserving written content.
